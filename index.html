Settlement rules for the KUBO World Cup pool, stated formally. Each market (Team to win, Top scorer) is settled independently using its own pot.

1 · Entries

Within a market, the entries are a finite list \[ e_i = (m_i,\; p_i,\; b_i), \qquad i = 1,\dots,n, \] where \(m_i\) is the member, \(p_i\) the pick (a team, or a player), and \(b_i \geq \$10\) the stake in dollars. Any amount at or above the \(\$10\) minimum is admissible, and the same pick may be backed by several members.

2 · The pot

All stakes in the market are pooled with no rake taken by the house: \[ P \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} b_i. \]

3 · Settlement

Let \(\omega\) be the realised outcome (the team that wins, resp. the actual top scorer), and let \[ W \;=\; \{\, i : p_i = \omega \,\}, \qquad S_W \;=\; \sum_{j \in W} b_j \] be the set of winning entries and their combined stake. Each entry is paid \[ \pi_i \;=\; \begin{cases} \displaystyle P \cdot \frac{b_i}{S_W}, & i \in W, \\[1.2em] 0, & i \notin W. \end{cases} \] Equivalently: the winners split the entire pot in proportion to their stakes. A lone correct backer (\(|W| = 1\)) has \(b_i / S_W = 1\) and so takes the whole pot.

If no entry is correct \((W = \varnothing)\), the market is void and every stake is refunded: \[ \pi_i = b_i \quad\text{for all } i. \]

4 · Conservation

Proposition. In either branch the pot is distributed exactly. When \(W \neq \varnothing\), \[ \sum_{i \in W} \pi_i \;=\; \frac{P}{S_W}\sum_{i \in W} b_i \;=\; \frac{P}{S_W}\, S_W \;=\; P, \] and when \(W = \varnothing\), \(\sum_i \pi_i = \sum_i b_i = P\) by refund. The house takes no rake. The net return to a winner is \[ \pi_i - b_i \;=\; b_i\!\left(\frac{P}{S_W} - 1\right), \] which is positive precisely when \(P > S_W\), i.e. whenever some stake was placed on a losing pick.

5 · Worked example

Brazil is backed by Alice (\(\$30\)) and Bob (\(\$10\)); a further \(\$60\) is spread across other teams. Then \(P = \$100\). Brazil wins, so \(W = \{\text{Alice}, \text{Bob}\}\) and \(S_W = \$40\):

\[ \pi_{\text{Alice}} = 100\cdot\tfrac{30}{40} = \$75, \qquad \pi_{\text{Bob}} = 100\cdot\tfrac{10}{40} = \$25. \]

Net: Alice \(+\$45\), Bob \(+\$15\); total paid \(=\$100=P\).

6 · Conventions

  • Deadline. Submissions close at the end of 16 June 2026. After the deadline both markets are locked and no further entries are accepted.
  • Two markets, two pots. Team-to-win and Top-scorer are settled independently, each with its own pot \(P\). Rules 1–5 apply within a single market.
  • Free entry. Each member may back any pick for any positive stake. Distinct members may back the same pick; if it wins, they share that pot by stake (rule 3).
  • Void market. If no entry in a market is correct, that market is void and all of its stakes are refunded (rule 3, final clause). The other market is unaffected.
  • Honour system. KUBO runs on trust: stakes are not collected up front and no money is escrowed. Settlement is voluntary — winners are paid out, and losing stakes are owed, in good faith. Payments are made by Venmo or Monzo.
